Detailed Description

Abstract Qt 6 QRhiWidget providing a hardware-accelerated Vulkan presentation canvas.

Implements QRhiWidget's rendering lifecycle (initialize, render, releaseResources) and provides stateful GPU recording primitives for textures, geometry buffers, and stencil tests.
