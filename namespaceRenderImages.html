Detailed Description

Central CPU-side pixmap registry bridging MuPDF worker threads and the Vulkan renderer.

MuPDF worker threads rasterize PDF pages and slices into QImage buffers, registering them here under unique RenderHandle identifiers. The GUI thread and VulkanCanvas query and consume these images during scene composition without requiring direct synchronization locks across the graphics driver or Vulkan command buffers.
